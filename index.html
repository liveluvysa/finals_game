HOW TO PLAY

Take turns placing marks. Get 3 in a row to win a round.
Vanishing marks: only 3 per player. A 4th mark deletes your oldest (it blinks as a warning).
Win 3 rounds to win the match.
BOOM: blow up an opponent’s mark, then place yours.
SHIELD: keep 4 marks this round.
DOUBLE MOVE: place two marks in one turn.

Each player gets ONE power-up per round. Power-ups refresh every new round!
